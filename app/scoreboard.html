THE SCOREBOARD

Every turn, MAG gives each option it could click a score, then plays the highest one. The score is just a sum: each measured thing about the board contributes a little push toward or away from that option. This page shows the whole sum, for every option, in real games. Nothing here is recomputed in your browser — the engine did the arithmetic and this page only draws it.

How to read this

A green bar is a reason to click the option; red is a reason not to. Each bar is one measured fact multiplied by how much that fact was found to matter, so the bars literally add up to the score. The gold-bordered option is the one MAG played. The percentage is what its score implies against the alternatives — on this page the bot is in best-move mode, so it always takes the top one, and the percentage tells you how close the call was.
