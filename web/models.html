Open sheets — the format ALAKAZAM starts in. Hidden information is exactly two things: what's in the back and the exact EV spread. XATU's full evidence-reading stays parked.
NOT ON THIS MAP, and that is a decision rather than an oversight. ROLES, WAR, NMF, COUNTERPLAY, COUNTERS, CORES, DYNAMICS, MEGA DEX, ILLUSION, SPECIES SETS, BRING PRIORS and SMOGON PRIORS each have their own entry in docs/MODELS.md and none of them has a box here. They are censuses, reports, data repairs and outside sources — they describe the format, or fix the dex, and in every case the thing that acts on them is already drawn. Each one is declared by name with its reason in tests/test-model-map.js, which fails if a model is added to the ledger and left off this picture, and fails just as hard if one of these is quietly dropped from the ledger instead. The full ledger is the place to read them.
One of the twelve carries an expiry date, and it is written into the declaration rather than left to be noticed. BRING PRIORS is off this map only because the one thing that would draw on it is GARY, and GARY's default opponent policy is the coin — so today nothing acts on p_lead or p_bring at all (docs/SEARCH.md R11). The day GARY is switched on, something decides off that artifact, it earns a box, and this paragraph becomes wrong.
