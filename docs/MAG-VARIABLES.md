# MAG — every variable, what it means, and why its weight is what it is

**2026-07-27** · 47 features · fitted on 82,836 human decisions from 3,340 clean open-sheet games

GENERATED by `build/build_mag_variables.js` from the live weight file. Do not hand-edit the tables.

Nobody typed these numbers. Each is fitted: the model is shown a real human decision and all the
options that person had, and the weights are nudged until the option they actually clicked comes
out most likely. So every weight answers one question — **what makes a human more likely to click
this** — and never *what wins*.

`ZERO` marks a weight whose 95% interval contains zero: the data cannot tell it from no effect.

The last column is the same fit with **popularity removed entirely**.

On held-out human decisions the full model picks the option the person actually clicked **30.9%** of the time; without popularity, **28.7%** — **a loss** of 2.2 points. So popularity carries real signal and is not crowding the board facts out. An earlier version of this document asserted the opposite, from a fit whose `DROP=` reached only half the corpus.
