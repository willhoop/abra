Will named the unifying cause: *"ITS PART OF THE MULTI TURN TAILWIND TYPE ANALYSIS."*

    corr( Protect residual , MULTI-TURN moves on the sheet ) = -0.343
    corr( Protect residual , INSTANT status moves          ) = +0.066   (nothing)

## 4. THE TWO REAL BUGS

**The mega identity gap, fixed universally.** A team sheet lists the **pre-mega** ability, so
`mon.ability` said Cursed Body for a Mega Gengar with Shadow Tag. `buildMon('mawile-mega')` already
returned "Huge Power" correctly — and `board.js` then **overwrote it with the sheet's "Intimidate"**.
Huge Power doubles Attack. **23,788 of 94,476 sheet slots (25.2%)** carry a mega stone whose forme
ability differs from the sheet's.

Fixed with **one resolver for the whole identity** — `board.js effective(mon, dex)` returns species,
ability, types, base stats and weight together, so a caller cannot hold the mega's ability beside the
base forme's types. Nothing is named; the ability derives from the dex's own `isMega` + `abilities`,
and `tests/test-effective-identity.js` proves it reproduces **all 62** checkable entries of
`medicham2-browser.js`'s hand-written `MEGA_ABIL` map. That map is now provably redundant.

**The fixture could not see any of this** — all 74 feature hashes were byte-identical across the
change, because no mega with a differing ability stands on its boards. That is why the guard is a
ratcheted fitness function (**234 raw reads across 40 files, may only shrink**) rather than another
fixture entry: the next regression is a different species.

**Two new features were node-only.** `board-data.js` carries an explicit allow-list and neither
ability `onResidual` nor condition `duration` was on it, so the browser scored both as always-zero
while node did not. **58 of 58 features now agree to 6 dp**, up from 56 of 58.

## 8. THE CEILING, SINCE IT WAS ASKED DIRECTLY

Will: *"I DON'T WANT TOP 10% I WANT A SOLVER THAT WILL ALWAYS WIN."*

That is unreachable, and this repository already measured why. `MODELS.md:230`: the 55% skill ceiling
was bot-contaminated; **clean it is 52.4%, and the interval includes 50.** In Reg M-B the
higher-rated player barely beats a coin. Damage rolls, accuracy, criticals, speed ties and
simultaneous commitment cap how much skill can express itself per game.

Deeper: a simultaneous-move game with a non-transitive component has its optimum in **mixed**
strategies, which lose some fraction of the time by construction. Perfect against perfect in a
symmetric matchup is 50%. Nash buys **unexploitable**, not unbeatable — `POKER-TO-POKEMON.md` §6.3
already says it.

**Where "always wins" IS literally achievable: `DUSK`**, the endgame exact solver (≤2v2, 1v1). Small
enough subgames are solvable and perfect play is computable, so it does always win when a win exists.
It is on the roadmap, unbuilt, and it is the only component where the word "solver" is accurate.

The realistic targets are: **unexploitable** globally (SLOWKING/Nash), **maximally exploitative**
against a read opponent (HYPNO, unbuilt), and **perfect** in solved endgames (DUSK, unbuilt).

## 9. HOW TO WORK, LEARNED THE HARD WAY TONIGHT

- **Measure the noise floor before believing an effect.** Split one arm's games in half, where the
  truth is 0.00 by construction, and see how big a difference the instrument invents. That killed
  three claims in minutes. `conditional_audit.js --calibrate` does it.
- **Check the corpus stamps before attributing an effect to a lever.** The 47.9% came from corpora
  differing in three ways. This was the single most common failure of the day.
- **A lever reading zero may never have been switched on.** Voluntary switches were exactly
  `4 x games` in every corpus.
- **The guards work — let them.** They caught me four separate times (a raw `MC.mons` index, three
  silent catches, a redundant raw read, a swallowed parse error). Fixing beats re-baselining:
  **silent catches went 239 -> 232, the first time that number has gone down.**
- **My predictions were wrong and my measurements were right,** consistently. The ability fix would
  shrink Blaziken's residual (it did not move a decimal). Contaminated data explained PORYGON2's
  ceiling (it did not). G2 passed at 40/40 (that was one lucky board; it is 25-40% failures).
