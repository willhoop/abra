## 1. WHAT THE BRIEF ASKED FOR, AND WHAT IT TURNED INTO

Three items were asked for. All three are done. The refit — item 1 — is not the interesting part.

**The hypothesis it was meant to test is REFUTED.** `allyHit` did not move: −0.0187 → −0.0126, about
0.2 standard errors. The immunity bug was not why it sat near zero. It sits near zero because humans
genuinely do not avoid hitting their own partner, and it should be reported that way. It is one of
four features whose interval contains zero.

**What the refit found instead was a much larger defect.** `fit_joint.js` matched a human's click by
requiring the move's TARGET to match, and `board.js` builds a spread candidate with `targetMon: null`
because Earthquake is not aimed. **No spread click could ever match.** Spread moves are 14.94% of all
human move clicks; 1,393 of 1,397 were discarded. The pair fit was estimating 74 weights from
**24,997 of 82,483 joint turns**, and the missing 70% was precisely the turns the pair model exists
to learn from.

Nine of eighteen pair weights changed sign. `spreadFreeBesideAlly` went **−4.986 → +0.859**.

**The corrected vector beats the shipped one at 70.9% of decisive pairs** [66.0, 75.4], replicated on
three disjoint seed blocks (66.7%, 65.9%, 70.9%) with `sprt.js` and `paired_h2h.js` agreeing to the
decimal. The bar is 55%.

**Roadmap item 1's stated premise is void.** It read −5.054 as evidence that the imitation objective
prices good play badly. That number was a fitter defect. The item may still be worth doing; this is
no longer evidence for it, and DODUO's 42.0% was measured on the contaminated vector.

## 3. THE PATTERN, AND IT IS THE MOST USEFUL THING HERE

> **Every expensive bug this session had one shape: something degrades, it counts itself correctly,
> and nothing fails.**

| defect | what the code already knew |
| --- | --- |
| the spread matcher | printed "57,486 of 82,483 unmatched" in every run |
| the forme lookup | `dmgFailures.unknownSpecies` counted every miss, for weeks |
| mega, four times | the mega rate was 0% |
| MEDICHAM's backtest | every forme silently filtered from its sample |

The counters were right. The numbers scrolled past. **A counter without a budget is a number; a
counter with a budget is a guard.**

`tests/test-degradation-budgets.js` is that guard. Ceilings are MEASURED (`--ratchet`), never chosen,
and may only be tightened. Fitters are read from the artifact each one writes rather than re-run,
because a fit takes half an hour and is the only honest reporter of what it saw.

The second pattern, from `docs/ARTIFACT-ACCESS-RULES.md`: **sharing an artifact is not enough; the
ACCESSOR has to be shared too.** Every engine already read the same `MC.mons` from the same generated
file. It broke anyway, because five places each wrote their own doorway into it — two correct, three
wrong, all five written by Claude in five different sessions.
