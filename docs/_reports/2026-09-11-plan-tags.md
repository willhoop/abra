## 1. Headline

**Zero of the 22 unconsumed tags is a missing mechanic.** Every in-scope one has an engine reader,
and 19 of the 22 already have a LIVE census probe. The count is wrong because **the detector is
wrong, three separate ways**:

1. **`tag_dex.js:476` greps for the tag's `probe:` string, and the engine reads by tag NAME.** Sixteen
   of the unconsumed tags are read through `TAGS.param('<kind>', id, '<tag>')` or `TAGS.has(...)`,
   and their `probe:` strings do not appear in the engine. Some probe strings are prose that could
   never appear in code, for example `'onDisableMove reads activeMoveActions and disables itself'`
   (`firstTurnOnly`) and `'minimize-flagged move into a minimized body'` (`punishesMinimize`). Others
   are dead names like `'naturalGift'` (`resistBerry`) and `'onSourceModifySecondaries'` (`hitsTwice`).
2. **The one existing lookup detector misses nested parentheses.** `tests/mutation_harness.js:682`
   (`sourceConsumers`) is `/TAGS\.(?:param|has|withTag|reactorsTo)\(([^;]{0,220}?)\)/` and stops at the
   first `)`. `m2:10255` is
   `TAGS.param('ability',(m.ability||'').replace(/[^a-z0-9]/g,''),'modifiesWeight')`, so
   `modifiesWeight` reads unconsumed in BOTH detectors while its census row is LIVE.
3. **`engine/coverage.js:330` dedupes a tag by its FIRST row.** `preventsStatDrop` counts as
   unconsumed only because its first row is the item Clear Amulet (`isNonstandard: 'Past'`, n=0),
   while its 12-member ability row is read (`m2:19594`, `m2:19619`) and has 7 LIVE probes. It fails
   the other way too: `survivesFromFull`'s ability row (Sturdy, 9 legal carriers) has
   `consumedBy: null`, but it is hidden behind the item row and counted as consumed. The engine does
   read it (`m2:38522`), so no mechanic is affected. The detector is simply blind in both directions.

**Verdicts for the 22:** 17 are CONSUMED ELSEWHERE (detector misses), 2 are VESTIGIAL/superseded, and
3 are VESTIGIAL with zero legal carriers.
**Verdicts for the 16:** 7 are in scope and need a probe (one of them is only a retag of three
existing LIVE rows), and 9 have zero legal members, so they are out of scope.
