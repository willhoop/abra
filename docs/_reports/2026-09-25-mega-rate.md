## 2. The counter (what was added)

- `solver/arena/mega_rate.js` — one definition, three consumers. `tally()` / `game(API, {A, B})` /
  `summary()` for MEDICHAM games. Capability is the engine's own `megaTargetFor(body)` at every decision,
  OFFERED is `canMegaNow`, CHOSE is a joint that asked for mega, and the mega is the side's `megaUsed` flag
  flipping over a step. Mismatches are counted: `chose_not_happened`, `happened_not_chose`,
  `mega_not_capable`. `parsedGame()` does the same for a parsed battle log (ROTOM). `humanRate()` does the
  whole dataset. `neverMega(bot)` is the deliberate break.
- `solver/arena/arena.js` — a per-bot `mega` block in every artifact: rate, Wilson CI, turn and delay
  histograms, the floor. A WARNING when a bot megas on 0 capable sides or its CI upper is under the floor.
  New bot kind: a league spec path (`solver/machamp/league/gen5.json`) through `solver/mew/agent.js`, so
  the self-play champion can sit in the arena; its fallbacks are reported (`counters.league_agent`).
  New break `ARENA_BREAK=nevermega`.
- `solver/mew/play.js` — the same counter per agent name in every shard summary (`mega.by_agent`), so
  MEW self-play and MACHAMP gate matches report it.
- `solver/rotom/rotom.js` — every per-game record in `games.jsonl` carries `mega: { p1, p2, mine }`
  (capable, capable_turn, mega, mega_turn, delay), from ROTOM's own copy of the log through the dataset's
  parser. The client summary carries the running rate, CI, the floor and `below_floor`.

**Cross-check of the two capability tests.** On 400 arena team pairs (3,200 brought bodies) the engine's

**ROTOM, end to end.** One local bo3 set (`run_local.js --sets 1 --a prior --b random --port 8797`,

Summaries: prior 2/2 capable megaed, random 1/2.

## 3. The floor (pre-registered before any bot was measured)

`floor = HUMAN_RATE − MARGIN = 0.9488 − 0.15 = 0.7988` (`solver/arena/mega_rate.js`). A bot FAILS when the
UPPER end of its Wilson 95% interval on capable sides is below the floor, so arena sample noise is already
allowed for. Fewer than 20 capable sides is CANNOT ANSWER, never a pass. At the test's ~25 capable sides a
bot must be below about 0.62 to fail: the historic 56% case fails; 70% does not. That is the intended
reach of "far below". A tighter bar needs more games per run.

`random` is reported and is NOT held to the floor. It megas at 0.689 [0.587, 0.775] and the arena warns.
