  is why the residual walk keeps corpses, and it stays that way.

  (`sim/battle-queue.ts:374`, which is the entrant's `runSwitch`). `setSpecies` sets it to the raw stored stat
  (`sim/pokemon.ts:1424`). Champions' `getActionSpeed` is `-speed` under Trick Room
  (`data/mods/champions/scripts.ts:46-54`).
