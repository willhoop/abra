# MAG v1 (per-slot action scorer) + DODUO v1 (joint two-slot coordinator), Reg M-C open sheets

2026-09-24. Solver stack, milestone M1 ("DODUO split out of MAG v0"). Historical findings record, not
maintained. Successor to `2026-09-23-policy-prior-v0.md`. Store-only: no simulator was used, so nothing
here waits on the Reg M-C MEDICHAM gate.

## Verdict

**Both beat prior v0 on held-out players, on every pre-set metric, with paired 95% CIs clear of zero.**
DODUO v1 (the full joint model) puts the human's exact joint action in its top 16 **87.2% [86.6, 87.7]**
of the time vs v0's **83.1%**; joint log-loss **2.730** vs **2.921** (paired −0.191 [−0.205, −0.179]).
MAG v1 *alone* — both slots scored independently, no pair term at all — already beats v0 *with* its pair
term (2.823, @16 85.7%). DODUO adds a clear, separate gain over MAG alone (−0.093 nats, +3.2 pp top-1).
The two weak spots both improved most, and are still the weakest: switch turns @16 **74.8 → 80.6%**,
turn 1 @16 **76.0 → 81.6%**.

Node reproduces the Python logits to **2.1e-14** on 76 held-out decisions (14,122 cells, every joint
status). The new test was shown RED on four deliberate breaks first. Dataset digest stamped in both model
files. Committed on branch `worktree-agent-ae26f145ebead8fac`; not merged, not pushed.

**Model.** MAG, per candidate, weights shared by both slots:
`x = [norm ctx; norm cand; E_move; E_species(target); E_species(me, ally, foeA, foeB); mean E(my 6); mean E(their 6); mean E(their revealed)]`,
`h1 = relu(W1 x)`, `h2 = relu(W2 [h1; mean over the slot's candidates of h1])`, `s = w·h2`. The pooled term
lets a candidate's score see its alternatives. DODUO, per valid joint cell:
`L_ij = s_a_i + s_b_j + r_a·h2_a_i + r_b·h2_b_j + u(h2_a_i)·v(h2_b_j) + wp·pair_ij + q·relu(Pa h2_a_i + Pb h2_b_j + Pp pair_ij + b)`.
The last term is a pairwise MLP — it can represent "attack the foe my partner is redirecting from",
"double switch", "one protects, one attacks", which v0's rank-8 bilinear term could only approximate. MAG
alone = the factorised product `s_a_i + s_b_j`. Trained together, loss = joint NLL + factorised NLL, so
MAG stays a valid standalone per-slot scorer. Labels, widening of hidden / uncertain targets and valid
cells are exactly v0's. 69,761 MAG + 21,254 DODUO parameters. H = 128, embeddings 16, rank 16, Q = 64,
AdamW 2e-3 one-cycle, batch 512, 12 epochs, best val at epoch 8 (val joint 2.451 vs v0's 2.611).

**Stack.** `torch 2.14.0+cpu` (pip, PyPI; not previously installed), numpy 2.5.1, 6 threads,
BelowNormal priority. Training wall-clock 3.7 h (machine shared; 0.5 s/step unloaded, ~1.1–2 s loaded).

## 5. Limits

1. **Still legal-looking, not legal**, and **opponent switch candidates are still the brought four**
   (hindsight for the opponent) — both inherited from v0's candidate set, unchanged on purpose so the
   comparison is exact. Use inside a bring world; intersect with MEDICHAM `legalActions`.
2. **Species identity adds hindsight nowhere new**: the sheets are open and "revealed" uses only mons seen
   so far. But the identity embeddings can memorise popular teams; the held-out-player split measures
   that honestly (a copied team crossing the split is the real ladder too).
3. **Node cost: 4.8 ms per decision** (v0 0.76 ms), one core, featurisation included. Fine for a pruner
   called once per side per world; a hot loop would want caching of MAG's per-slot pass across worlds.
4. One training run, one seed. Val had plateaued by epoch 8 (2.451, 2.454, 2.455 after); train loss kept
   falling (2.21), so more capacity needs more regularisation, not more epochs.
5. A species absent from the whole dataset maps to the "rare" row in Node; the trainer never saw one.
6. The exact-decision subset is not a random sample (v0 §5 applies unchanged).
