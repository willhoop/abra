## 2. WHAT EACH MECHANISM WAS, AND WHAT ITS PROBE DID RED-FIRST

Every one was **confirmed at the line before a byte moved**, and in two of the three the card's stated
cause was refuted in part.

### M1 — the multi-accuracy volley. `tests/probe_multiaccuracy_address.js`

**Authority:** `data/mods/champions/scripts.ts:482-506`, inside `hitStepMoveHitLoop` — Champions
OVERRIDES that method (`:428`), so `sim/battle-actions.ts` is a different game and is not the citation.
Arrivals 2..n each take one `randomChance(accuracy, 100)` and the first failure ends the volley, where
`accuracy` is the move's REAL accuracy after the stages, `runEvent('ModifyAccuracy')` and
`runEvent('Accuracy')`.

**The card's cause was WRONG and the symptom was right.** The card claimed the per-arrival draw "takes a
different shared address on the two engines". It does not: the probe reads both address logs and finds
**11 of 11 shared**, character for character. What differs is the VALUE the accuracy is compared
against — this engine used the printed 90 off the tag and ignored every accuracy modifier in the game.

**Proved arithmetically on four of the six games before any edit**, from the shared addresses' own
values:

| game | address | u | ours (90) | authority | observed `-hitcount` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `…\|6\|any\|tripleaxel\|p10\|0`, GRAVITY | 0.95014 | miss -> 1 | 150 -> hit | us 1, SD 3 |
| 19 | `…\|1\|any\|populationbomb\|p10\|0`, Wide Lens | 0.98998 | miss -> 1 | 99 -> hit | us 1, SD 7 |
| 21 | `…\|2\|any\|populationbomb\|p21\|0..3` | .293/.643/.673/.9027 | stop at 4 | 99 -> hit | us 4, SD 5 |
| 31 | `…\|2\|any\|populationbomb\|p20\|0` | 0.94214 | miss -> 1 | 99 -> hit | us 1, SD 6 |

All eight of those `-hitcount` numbers are the observed ones in `data/_diag77-cards.json`.

**Fix:** `rollHitsOf` takes the accuracy `hitChance` computed for this attacker into this target on this
field — the same number the move's own to-hit step rolled against — cached on the row by `_stepAccuracy`
and read at `_stepDamage`. One accuracy authority, not two. The draw is still taken when the move cannot
miss (accuracy 150 still consumes its die in the authority); only `accuracy === true` (this engine's
`Infinity`, i.e. `!accMustRoll`) skips it.

**Probe, red-first with `MEDI_MULTIACC_RAW_ACC=1`:** a Wide Lens Maushold and a Wide Lens Weavile on
turns and slots whose shared value falls in `[0.90, 0.99)`.

- clean: authority `10 / 3`, medicham2 `10 / 3` — **green**
- knob armed: authority `10`, medicham2 `1` — **red**
- **control** (identical script, no Wide Lens): `1 / 1` on both engines in **both** knob states, and the
  Wide Lens is asserted to have MOVED the authority's own counts, so a green §4 cannot be a free pass.
- §0 derives the member set from the format on every run and refuses to continue if it is not
  `populationbomb, tripleaxel`.

### M3 — a non-permanent forme is not reverted when the body leaves. `tests/probe_nonpermanent_forme_revert.js`

**Authority, both lines re-opened:** `sim/pokemon.ts:1447` writes `this.baseSpecies = rawSpecies` ONLY
`if (isPermanent)`; `sim/pokemon.ts:1564` closes `clearVolatile()` with `setSpecies(this.baseSpecies)`,
which is `setType(species.types, true)` (:1398) — species and types in one call. §0 of the probe PARSES
every `formeChange(` in `data/abilities.ts`, keeps the abilities with a legal carrier, and prints which
pass `isPermanent`: `hungerswitch:1891` and `stancechange:4521` pass nothing, `forecast:1486` passes an
explicit `false`, `zerotohero:5621` passes `true`.

**The card's cause was RIGHT for one carrier of three.** Run red-first, the probe found Morpeko and
Castform **already IDENTICAL** on the switch-out road and only **Aegislash DIFFERS**
(`p2.party.aegislash.species  ours "aegislashblade" / authority "aegislash"`). The switch-out revert
existed and only the `formeCycleResidual` door stamped `_formeTempBase`; the Stance Change door did not.

**Fix, two parts:**
1. `stampTempForme(m, how, name)` — one implementation, called by both doors. Stance Change stamps
   `'swap'` (its two formes have different base stats, so the revert must REBUILD, not rename).
2. `revertTempFormeOnLeave(m, where)` — and it is called from `noteFaint` as well as `switchOut`.
   `faintMessages()` calls `clearVolatile(false)` on the corpse, and a faint does not go through this
   engine's `switchOut`. That is the statement `typesRestoredOnFaint`'s own header explicitly declined to
   make ("NOT CLAIMED HERE ... reverts a NON-PERMANENT forme"). **This is the half that moved Morpeko,
   which I had predicted would not move.**

**Probe, red-first with `MEDI_NO_TEMP_FORME_REVERT=1` (and `MEDI_FORECAST_NO_SWITCHOUT_REVERT=1` for
the Castform arm, which has its own older knob and is not governed by the new one):**

| board | clean | knob armed |
|---|---|---|
| morpeko-flips-then-pivots | IDENTICAL | DIFFERS `morpekohangry / morpeko` |
| aegislash-blade-then-pivots | IDENTICAL | DIFFERS `aegislashblade / aegislash` |

### M4 — the choice lock is never cleared. `tests/probe_choicelock_cleared.js`

**Authority:** `data/conditions.ts:349-353`. The probe greps `data/mods/champions/conditions.ts` on
every run and asserts it carries no `choicelock` row, so mainline IS the authority here.

```
onDisableMove(pokemon) {
  if (!pokemon.getItem().isChoice || !pokemon.hasMove(this.effectState.move)) {
    pokemon.removeVolatile('choicelock'); return;
  }
  if (pokemon.ignoringItem() || pokemon.volatiles['dynamax']) return;
  ... disable every other slot ...
}
```

`DisableMove` is raised from `Pokemon#getMoveRequestData`, i.e. **every time a move request is built**,
which is the foot of every turn. This engine destroyed the lock LAZILY inside `lockStillBinds` (which
runs when something asks the menu), and the `hasMove` half destroyed nothing at all — it only hid the
lock from `lockMenuMove`. `engine/board_state.js:1023` reads the RAW field, correctly, and its own
header had already PREDICTED this in as many words: *"If those two moments straddle a turn boundary, a
Knocked-Off Scarf leaves a lock standing on one side and not the other. That is an engine finding to
FILE."*

**Fix:** `choiceLockRequestSweep(bodies)`, called inside the turn block below `refill()` — the
authority's own `makeRequest('move')` moment. It is INSIDE the block deliberately: every `break _TURN`
is a `sideWiped`, and a turn that wipes a side never builds a request, so a sweep on the outer exit
would clear a lock the authority leaves standing. That is the mistake the `stall` expiry made and had to
reverse the same day.

**Probe, red-first with `MEDI_NO_CHOICELOCK_REQUEST_SWEEP=1`:**

| board | clean | knob armed |
|---|---|---|
| scarf-knocked-off-drops-the-lock | IDENTICAL | DIFFERS `vol.choicelock ours "iceshard" / authority ""` |
| scarf-locked-into-transform-drops-the-lock | IDENTICAL | DIFFERS `ours "transform" / authority ""`, plus the downstream `pp[].protect` and `stall` the lock caused |
| **scarf-keeps-its-lock (CONTROL)** | IDENTICAL | **IDENTICAL** |

§2 of that probe additionally reads the authority's OWN `volatiles.choicelock.move` off its battle
object on the control board and asserts it is `iceshard`, because "both engines report no lock" is also
what two engines with no choice lock at all look like. §0 derives that **exactly one** `isChoice` item is
legal here (Band and Specs are `isNonstandard: 'Past'`).
