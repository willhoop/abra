## THE THREE DEFECTS

### 1. A TRANSFORM SURVIVED A FAINT — the UNCAUSED game

`seed ...2654316929 vs ...2654486674`, turn 6, `protocol_diverged_at_turn: null`:

```
p1.party.ditto.species   medicham "metagross"       showdown "ditto"
p1.party.ditto.types     medicham "psychic/steel"   showdown "normal"
```

Replayed with `engine/replay_one.js`: the Ditto led, copied a Metagross with Imposter, fought as one
for five turns and then **fainted**. It never switched out.

**The revert already existed and was on one door of two.** `imposterRevert()` has been called from the
switch-out path since ROADMAP #95/#139. The authority has one function on two doors:

```
sim/pokemon.ts:1253    switch out    -> clearVolatile()
sim/battle.ts:2560     faintMessages -> pokemon.clearVolatile(false)   on the corpse
sim/pokemon.ts:1566    clearVolatile() ends with this.setSpecies(this.baseSpecies)
```

`engine/board_state.js` deliberately does NOT hold `species`, `maxhp` and `types` on a corpse — its own
header says *"a body arriving as a different Pokemon is the one thing on a corpse a later board can
still be wrong about"* — so this is board-material by the comparator's own design.

**IT WAS PUT ON `queueFaint` FIRST AND THE PROBE STAYED RED.** `queueFaint` is where the `_ttmWrap`
clear lives, citing this same `clearVolatile(false)` call, and it looked like the shared door. It is
not: only a handful of sites were converted to it and **Memento's self-KO is one of the twenty-odd that
still flip `fainted` inline**. The one call every faint site actually makes is `noteFaint`, and that is
where it went. Found by the arm reading `chandelure` in both the default and the control — the
"identical output across a varied knob" shape pointing at the fix rather than at the knob.

**ONE HANDLER LEGITIMATELY READS THE COPIED BODY AND IT IS ANSWERED, NOT ACCEPTED.** `receiverSweep`
reads `dead.ability`, and `runEvent('Faint')` (:2549) fires ABOVE the clear (:2560) — so a Receiver
inherits the ability a transformed corpse was WEARING. A bare revert hands it `imposter`, whose own
`refusesCopy.noreceiver` then refuses the inherit: a mechanic that works today would have silently
stopped. `_abAtFaint` is stamped in `noteFaint` and read by the sweep.

Knob `MEDI_TRANSFORM_SURVIVES_FAINT=1`. Counter `MEDSEEN.transformRevertedOnFaint`, counted apart from
`transformReverted` so a cold door is readable.
Probe: `tests/probe_transform_faint_revert.js`.

### 2. A POST-FAINT REPLACEMENT'S ENTRY DROP HAD NO HERB PASS

`seed ...2654016071 vs ...2654363031`, turn 4:

```
p2.party.sneasler.item        medicham "whiteherb"   showdown ""
p2.party.sneasler.boosts.atk  medicham -1            showdown 0
```

A Glimmora died at the residual, both sides sent replacements, one was a Staraptor, and its Intimidate
dropped a Sneasler holding a White Herb. The authority spends the herb before it closes the turn:

```
SD   |-unboost|p2a: Sneasler|atk|1  ->  |-enditem|p2a: Sneasler|White Herb  ->  |turn|5
US   |-unboost|p2a: Sneasler|atk|1  ->  |turn|5  ->  |-enditem|p2a: Sneasler|whiteherb
```

**Three doors of four were already wired.** ROADMAP #81 WIRE 11 wired all four of `whiteherb`'s
handlers — `onAnySwitchIn` (priority -2), `onAnyAfterMega`, `onAnyAfterMove`, `onResidual` — as the
lead pass, the mega, `_updateAll` and the residual walk. **None of those reaches `refill()`**: the
switch request that fills a dead body's slot is issued below the residual and below the last action
(sim/battle.ts:2909). `restoreStatsAll(actA, actB)` now runs after the refill's whole entry walk —
after, not per entrant, for the same priority -2 reason the lead site gives.

Beyond the announcement: `unburden.onAfterUseItem` doubles Speed the moment the item is spent, so the
herb coming off is a SPEED TIER CHANGE. Until this fix a replacement's Intimidate handed that back a
turn late, and Sneasler's own usage item IS a White Herb.

Knob `MEDI_REFILL_NO_HERB=1`. Counter `MEDSEEN.refillHerbPass`.
Probe: `tests/probe_refill_entry_herb.js`.

### 3. RECOIL WAS A SHARE OF WHAT WAS ROLLED, NOT OF WHAT WAS DEALT

`seed ...2653843264 vs ...2653816206`, turn 8 — one HP:

```
|-damage|p2a: Incineroar|20/170 par|[from] Recoil      showdown
|-damage|p2a: Incineroar|19/170 par|[from] Recoil      medicham2
```

A Flare Blitz into a full-HP Whimsicott holding a Focus Sash. The sash cut the damage to 134 and the
authority charged 33% of 134 = 44. This engine charged 33% of 135 = 45. The same Incineroar's Flare
Blitz into an unsashed Farigiraf on the previous turn agreed exactly.

**The arithmetic was already right and the INPUT was wrong.** ROADMAP #81 WIRE 4 fixed the formula on
this exact line. What it did not touch is where `dealt` is read:

```
medicham2   dealt += Math.min(dmg, tg.curHP)     <- captured ~270 lines ABOVE the clamps
authority   move.totalDamage += damage[i]        <- battle-actions.ts:965, the POST-handler value
```

Three handlers rewrite `dmg` between those points. `_reDealt()` re-reads it from the two that are
survival clamps (Endure, the `survivesFromFull` family) and **deliberately not from the Disguise
absorb**: Disguise's `onDamage` RETURNS 0, so `damage[i]` is 0 and the busted forme's chip is a
separate `damage()` call the recoil never sees. Re-reading there would newly charge recoil on a hit the
authority charges nothing for.

This was invisible to `tests/test-engine-diff.js` by construction — 0 of 6000 at all sixteen corners
today and before — because that instrument asks about the HIT and never about what the hit COSTS.

Knob `MEDI_DEALT_BEFORE_CLAMP=1`. Counter `MEDSEEN.dealtReReadAfterClamp`.
Probe: `tests/probe_recoil_after_clamp.js`.

## THE FOURTH IS NOT A DEFECT — AN UNSHARED DIE, AND THE INSTRUMENT SAYS SO

`seed ...2635122796 vs ...2634861011`, turn 2:

```
p2.party.staraptor.hp    medicham 160   showdown  87
p2.party.incineroar.hp   medicham 106   showdown 170
```

The harness sent Showdown `"switch 3, move 2"` — **a move with NO target**, because `chooseAction` and
`scripted` deliberately name no target for the `randomNormal` family. Showdown then ignores any
targetLoc it was given:

```
if (move.target !== 'randomNormal' && this.validTargetLoc(...))    sim/battle.ts, getTarget
```

and falls through to `getRandomTarget`. Outrage's target is drawn by each engine from its own PRNG,
and the arm's shared streams are `acc / crit / sec / dmg / stall` — **there is no sixth category for a
target draw**. Both engines are correct; they aimed at different bodies.

The instrument already knows. This run reports `VOID (instrument desync): 1 of 961`, `low-identity: 1
game`, and every unshared address shape is Outrage:

```
'acc outrage [sd only]': 2   'crit outrage [sd only]': 2   'dmg outrage [sd only]': 2
'acc outrage [me only]': 1   'crit outrage [me only]': 1   'dmg outrage [me only]': 1
unshared_address_field_rollup: { 'target differs': 3, 'turn differs': 3 }
```

**FILED, NOT FIXED, AND IT IS NOT ENGINE'S TO FIX.** Sharing the draw means adding a sixth arm category
to `engine/game_differential.js`, which is the instrument and is not this division's file. Doing it
changes every arm's dice and therefore every published rate. Handed up as a decision.

## SEVEN FIXTURE ERRORS IN THREE PROBES, EVERY ONE FOUND BY READING A COUNTER

Recorded because this is the failure this division keeps paying for.

1. **`finalgambit` chosen as the "zero-damage" self-KO** — it reads `basePower: 0` and deals the user's
   whole HP bar. Filtered on the `fixedDamage` tag, not on the number.
2. **A `null` script slot** — Showdown REFUSES a pass from a body that can act
   (*"Can't pass: Your Abomasnow must make a move"*). The game threw.
3. **The victim's derived "self-targeting status move" was PROTECT** — Protect's own target is `self`,
   so a bare walk hands the victim a shield against the thing under test. Filtered on `stallingMove`.
4. **...and then it was BATON PASS** — a self-targeting status move that takes the body off the field,
   ending the fixture a turn early. Filtered on `selfSwitch`.
5. **The herb holder clicked a self-BOOST move**, raising the very stat the drop then lowered, so the
   stage never went negative and the herb had nothing to answer. Both engines agreed and the arm was
   green while staging nothing.
6. **The measured boundary index was TYPED and was off by one** — `onBoundary` fires once for the board
   BEFORE turn 1 as well as after each turn, so boundary 1 was a turn on which nobody had died. The
   probe reported "the arm did not happen" against a fixture in which it had. It now FINDS the boundary
   by looking for the carrier on the field.
7. **The fix was placed on `queueFaint`, which is not the shared door** — see defect 1.

Not one of these was caught by an arm going red. All seven were caught by the probes' own
"the arm must have happened" assertions and by reading printed counters.
