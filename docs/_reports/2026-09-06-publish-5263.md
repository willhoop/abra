## The one figure that disagreed with the brief

**The brief said narration 71. The clause reads 70.**

Both numbers are in the artifact and they are different quantities.
`end_state[0].summary.by_cause_totals.games_narration_only` is **71** — RAW.
`engine/quarantine.js` subtracts the one declared row — the perish drain sitting above `|upkeep|`
where the authority puts it below, closeted by Will 2026-08-28 — before stating the clause, and `status.js`
prints:

```
NARRATION-ONLY: 70 of 961 = 7.3% ... across 69 cause(s)
(71 narration-only raw, less 1 declared and 0 cleared on decision impact)
```

`docs/ENGINE.md`'s batch F account already said 70. The brief was quoting the raw. The living
documents publish **70** and state the raw beside it.

This is the same shape as the trap the brief itself warned about one field over:
`by_cause_totals.games_board_material` reads **29**, which is the by-cause attribution over the 100
protocol-diverged games and is not the clause quantity. The clause is **34**. The difference is
exactly `state.board_parted_before_the_protocol_did` = **5** — games that part a board while the
protocol never diverges, so they can carry no protocol cause and appear in no by-cause table.
29 + 5 = 34, and `by_cause_reconciles` is `true`.

So the artifact publishes four plausible readings of "how many games are wrong" — 34, 29, 71, 70 —
and only two of them are clauses. Read the operands, never the summary total.

Everything else in the brief reproduced exactly: board-material 34, protocol 100, release
`d9e551ed0d5a`, cap 20, arm `middle`, `empirical-click/v1`, `--team-store data/team-pool-frozen`,
census 829/829/0, and the gate at 7 of 9 (equivalently 1 of 8 gating — the two are never quoted
