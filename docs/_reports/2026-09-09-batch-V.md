### The fix

`engine/medicham2-browser.js`, the substitute branch inside `_stepApply`:

- **The arrivals are SKIPPED, not SLICED.** `R.pkIdx[i]`, `R.crits[i]` and `R.reprice(crit, i)` are
  all keyed on the ORIGINAL arrival number — Parental Bond's arrival 2 and Triple Axel's arrival 3
  have their own base power — so the packet vector is left whole and the body loop starts at a new
  `R.pkFrom`. Slicing would have silently re-numbered all three.
- **The doll's line is emitted per arrival**, which is the same `if` the authority has: every arrival
  the doll survives writes `-activate|[damage]`, the one that breaks it writes `-end`.
- **The clamp is per arrival**, against the doll's HP *at that moment* (`data/moves.ts:18341-18344`),
  and `_absorbed` is the sum. For a single-packet click that is character-for-character the old
  `_reDealt(dmg, _s0)`, so every pre-existing arm is byte-identical.
- **`_dollPaid` is an OFFSET inside `_reDealt`, not a fourth call site.** A broken-through row has two
  payments in it and every later clamp — Endure, the Focus Sash, the arrival re-price — speaks about
  the BODY's half alone. Folding the doll's half into the helper means those three sites are untouched
  and cannot forget it. It is 0 on every road with no doll in it.
- **The doll's drain is paid at the doll's own site** and the foot call pays `_rowDealt - _dollPaid`,
  so a volley whose packets later COLLAPSE under a second clamp cannot heal the attacker twice.
- **`_landed` starts at `R.pkFrom`**, because the authority's `-hitcount` is `hit - 1` and its `hit`
  counter does not care what absorbed the arrival. That also gives Rage Fist the right arrival count.
- **`MEDFAILS.timesHitSubstituteUncounted` got NARROWER because the road changed**, not because the
  counter was relaxed: a volley that breaks through now reaches the counter site, so its doll arrivals
  ARE counted. What still misses is the click a doll ate whole, which is what the bump now measures.

New counters: `MEDSEEN.subVolleyArrivalsAbsorbed`, `.subVolleyBrokeThrough`, `.subVolleyArrivalsOnBody`.

### AND A SECOND LINE IN THE SAME ROOT: `-hitcount` OVER A ROW THE DOLL DROPPED

`-hitcount` is not a per-target step in the authority at all — it is one line in
`hitStepMoveHitLoop`, below the loop and below `faintMessages`, firing on `move.multihit` alone
(`scripts.ts:547-551`). A row dropped by `HIT_SUBSTITUTE` is dropped inside `spreadMoveHit`'s copy and
never reaches that condition, so the count is announced anyway. This engine's `R.out` dropped the row
before `_stepHitCount`. **Measured, not reasoned:** the DOLL-HOLDS and BREAK-LAST arms read authority
`2` against our `null`.

`_stepHitCount.runsWhenOut = true` and one clause in `_walk`. **A flag on the step, not a flush below
the driver:** the position of that line against `_stepFaint`, `_stepAfterFaint` and
`_stepHpThresholdBoost` is the whole of two earlier wires, and a flush below the walk would move it
past all three. `R.hitLanded` is only ever set inside `_stepApply` and nothing below `_stepApply` sets
`R.out`, so no other refusal can reach the step through the gap. The COLLAPSE road keeps its existing
refusal — a click whose packets could not be addressed does not know its own count, and writing `1`
would be an invented number.

**AND THE OBVIOUS FIX CANNOT WORK, WHICH IS THE PART THREE BATCHES HAD NOT ESTABLISHED.** "Move only

## 4. THE QUESTION THE BRIEF ASKED: **HOW MANY MORE CARDS LIKE THIS ARE THERE?**

### The method, stated

A divergence is a difference in the LINE STREAM. **It can only be a board difference if the two
engines' streams differ in the MULTISET of STATE-BEARING lines, not merely in their order.** An
`ordering` divergence is a permutation: the same state is created and destroyed on both engines and
the boards reconverge. A line one engine writes and the other does not is a state one engine has and
the other does not.

So, over the divergence window of every NARRATION-ONLY game in the AFTER dump, canonicalised
(whitespace, case, `[of]` dropped everywhere and `[from]` dropped on boost lines, which is the
differential's own `stat-attribution` rule), the state-bearing prefixes being
`-damage -heal -boost -unboost -status -curestatus faint -enditem -item -sethp -setboost -swapboost
-clearboost -formechange detailschange -transform -start -end -sidestart -sideend -weather
-fieldstart -fieldend -terastallize -mega`.

**Its bound, stated:** the window is the 10 lines the dump keeps after the divergence point
(`engine/game_differential.js:4391`). A permutation spanning more than 10 lines would read as a
difference, and a difference that only shows beyond 10 lines would read as a permutation. It is a
rough count and the brief asked for one.

### The answer

**16 of 18 are permutations. 2 differ.** Plus the one this batch fixed, that is

> **3 of the 19 NARRATION-ONLY causes that existed at the start of this batch — about one in six —
> carry a state-bearing line one engine writes and the other does not.**

Both survivors have the same shape as the Dual Wingbeat card: the body that differed stopped
mattering before any turn boundary was sampled.

**A — the bounced Sleep Powder** (`|-miss|p1a|p2a <> |-status|p2a|slp|[from]sleeppowder`). Hatterene's
Magic Bounce reflects Vivillon's Sleep Powder; the authority's bounced copy MISSES and **ours lands**,
so Vivillon is ASLEEP on our board and awake on the authority's. It reads narration-only because
Vivillon dies to a Dazzling Gleam later in the same turn on BOTH engines. Batch U named this as *"a new
observation, not a finding"*; it is now measured as a board difference.

**B — Instruct and the perish counter** (`|-fail|p2a <> |-singleturn|p1a|instruct`). The authority
REFUSES the Instruct; ours executes it. Downstream in the same turn the authority's Perish Song starts
`perish1` on **both** foes and ours on only one — `|-start|p2a: Oranguru|perish1` is missing here — and
ours additionally writes `|cant|p1a: Gengar|nopp|protect`, a PP difference. Neither perish nor sleep is
in `end_state_not_compared`, so both WOULD be visible at a boundary; they were erased before one.

### THE CLASSIFIER OVER-FIRED FIRST, AND IT IS REPORTED RATHER THAN QUIETLY TUNED

Before the `[from]`-on-boost rule was applied it reported **4** differing, and the two extras were the
same line on both engines wearing different attribution — ours `|-boost|…|def|1|[from] ability: stamina`
against the authority's bare `|-boost|…|def|1`, which the differential already normalises away. A
classifier tuned after seeing its own answer is worth stating: this one was, and the tuning is the
differential's own published rule rather than a judgement made to reach a number.

### WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE HEADLINE

`BOARD-MATERIAL 0 of 958` means **no board difference survived to a compared turn boundary.** It does
not mean none occurred, and the artifact already publishes that bound about itself. On this sample the
gap between the two readings is **about 3 causes**, all three erased within the turn that produced
them. **6.0.0 should say so in those words.**
