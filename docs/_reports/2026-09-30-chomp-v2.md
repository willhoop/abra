The break (`CHOMP2_BREAK=shuffle`, v2's labels shuffled) gives +0.0312 [+0.0191, …], FAIL as required.

**What the result means.**
- The field block buys 0.0019 of VAL log-loss (0.65140 against v1's 0.65326), and on TEST that gain does not separate
  from zero.
- The spread makes things worse on VAL. That is expected, and it was pre-registered as a caveat: the simulated
  outcomes (T1, T2) were played at the engine table's per-species stat line (`solver/arena/teams.js` `buildBody`, which
  is `buildMon`'s `st`, not a uniform spread). So set-spread facts describe bodies those games never fielded.
- Only T3 (human) was played at real spreads, and it is 188 games with no bring signal (τ_human 0.32, as in v1).
- **A spread arm can only be tested fairly once the arena fields set spreads.** Until then the offline data cannot
  reward it.

## 4. Where the path goes

- The field block is the part that carries information (it is what VAL selected), but it is not yet enough to beat
  v1 on held-out outcomes.
- Two levers are honest next steps. Neither is taken here.
  1. Make the arena field set spreads. That changes every arena figure, so it is a new basis and a Will call.
  2. More targeted samples where the field matters (setter teams), so the field weights are identified.
