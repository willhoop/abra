# PORYGON2 v0 — the value net (2026-09-24)

**Verdict.** V0 passes. On held-out players, PORYGON2 v0 beats the count-HP logistic and the
embeddings-only net on log-loss and Brier. Every paired, game-clustered 95% CI is clear of zero, and the
margin over the embeddings-only net is larger than the split-half floor. So the engine facts earn their
place, except on turn 1. **V2 is not shown.** In the PRE-GATE arena, MILTANK with the PORYGON2 leaf ties
MILTANK with the heuristic leaf when both are at depth 0: 0.515 (0.446–0.583) over 200 games at 1 s.
Against MILTANK's default (depth-2 random rollouts, then the heuristic) it scores 0.605 (0.536–0.670).
That gain is confounded with dropping the random rollout turns (§4).

Everything that reads MEDICHAM is **PRE-GATE**, because the Reg M-C gate is not open. That covers the
damage-race facts inside the full net and both arena matches. The embeddings-only net and the count-HP
baseline are store-only.

## 1. What was built (`solver/porygon2/`)

| file | what |
|---|---|
| `features.js` | position → tokens (6 per side), side, field, **engine facts** (10 per direction). A single `encode()` is used both for the training data and for the leaf |
| `build_dataset.js` | human Reg M-C games → one row per public position (185,480), plus split codes for both players |
| `train.py` | the count-HP logistic, the embeddings-only net and the full net on the same rows. Also clustered CIs, results by turn, the split-half floor, export, and the agreement fixture |
| `infer.js` | a hand-written float64 forward pass that reads the exported JSON |
| `leaf.js` | MEDICHAM battle → `prior_adapter.publicState` → `encode` → net |
| `bench.js` | leaf cost per position |
| `model/porygon2-v0{,-emb}.json`, `.metrics.json` | weights (base64 float32) and every figure below |

MILTANK wiring: `solver/miltank/{rollout,cells,search}.js`. The leaf mode travels in the job (`o.leaf`,
otherwise env `MILTANK_LEAF`, otherwise heuristic), so pool workers use the leaf their parent asked for.
The new counter is `leafPory2`. In the arena, `--leaf-x/--leaf-y/--depth-x/--depth-y` set the leaf and
the depth for each bot.

**Inputs.**
- **Tokens.** One token per sheet mon. It carries learned species, item and ability embeddings, and the
  mean of the four move embeddings. The public state adds seen, active, fainted, HP, status, boosts,
  mega, item-lost and five volatiles. The current forme adds base stats and types. An unseen sheet mon
  has weight p = (4 − seen)/(6 − seen).
- **Pooling.** Deep Sets: a weighted sum, a max and an actives-sum. The logit is
  `g(A,B) − g(B,A)`, so the net is antisymmetric by construction.
- **Engine facts.** Each fact is computed for every live-active pair from MEDICHAM's `dmgRange`,
  `hitProb`, `effSpeed` and `movePriority`, with Trick Room flipping the order. The facts are: can KO
  (max roll), sure KO (min roll), mean and max expected-hit fraction, who is faster, who kills first
  (priority then speed), KO with priority, turns-to-clear over all remaining foes, and remaining foes
  one active can KO.
- **Bodies.** Bodies are built as the arena builds them. Every body gets the table's flat spread, and
  no nature is applied, because the sheets carry no spreads.

**Split.** MAG/DODUO's salt and rule, by player. A value label belongs to the whole game, and the net is
antisymmetric, so the same game seen from the other side is the same example. For that reason, training
uses only games where **both** players are train players: 17,285 games and 119,334 positions. 8,615
games that mix a train player with a held-out player are not trained on.
- Test: every position seen by a test player. That is 36,083 positions, 37,829 views and 5,220 games.
- Val: positions seen by a val player, used for early stopping only.

**Calibration** of the full net, in 10 bins over the test views. Predictions near 50% match the observed
win rate to about 1–2 points. The most confident bins are slightly under-confident: 0.849 predicted
against 0.879 won, and 0.957 against 0.965.

**Honest limits.**
- **Overfitting.** Validation loss is lowest after the first epoch, and after that the net memorises
  games (train 0.48, val rising). V0 is that epoch-0 model. Stronger regularisation, chosen on val, is
  the obvious next step.
- **Noisy labels.** The labels are raw outcomes of human games, and 41% of the games in this corpus end
  in a forfeit. Label noise is the ceiling the learning report predicted.
- **What the facts leave out.**
  - Focus Sash and the other survival effects are not in the "can KO" fact.
  - Spread damage is the engine's spread flag.
  - No accuracy is folded into the max roll.
  - Unseen foes are weighted uniformly.

## 3. Node ↔ Python, and cost

- `solver/tests/test-porygon2.js` is GREEN at 423/423.
  - 24 raw held-out positions are re-encoded in Node, engine facts included. The worst logit difference
    against Python's float64 is 8.9e-16 for the full net and 1.8e-15 for the embeddings-only net.
  - Antisymmetry holds exactly (difference 0).
  - Sheet-permutation and active-swap invariance hold.
  - The Trick Room flip was checked on 23 positions.
  - The metrics' claim is re-read from the artifact.
  - Leaf wiring is checked with counters.
- Each deliberate break turns it RED: `PORY2_BREAK=slot` (354/423), `speed` (401/423), `facts`
  (398/400), `PORY2_INFER_BREAK=pool` (421/423), `MILTANK_BREAK=leaf` (422/423).
- **Leaf cost** (`solver/out/porygon2/bench.json`): 174 arena positions, lean copy, one thread.

  | part | median µs | mean µs | p95 µs |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | total | 847 | 820 | 1,299 |
  | public state | 38 | — | — |
  | encode (83 `dmgRange` calls per position) | 641 | — | — |
  | forward pass | 158 | — | — |

  The engine facts, not the net, are about 75% of the cost. That is the first thing to cut if MILTANK
  needs more cells.
- Size: the net has 41,241 parameters. The tracked files grow by about 0.6 MB: two models at 238 KB and
  231 KB, a 117 KB fixture and 20 KB of metrics.

## 4. V2 shakedown — MILTANK + PORYGON2 vs MILTANK + heuristic (PRE-GATE)

These run on the live tree, not a frozen release. Both sides run MILTANK v1, with k 8×8, 4 workers,
1 s per decision, the paired seating and real human teams.

| match | score (PORYGON2 arm) | Wilson 95% | team pairs both/split/lost | median playouts / decision (P2 vs heur) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| depth 0 both (a pure leaf swap after the current turn), seed 1, `solver/out/arena/pory2-d0-vs-heur-d0-g200-s1.json` | **0.515** (103–97) | 0.446–0.583 | 21 / 61 / 18 | 191 vs 344 |
| PORYGON2 depth 0 vs heuristic depth 2, seed 2, `…pory2-d0-vs-heur-d2-g200-s2.json` | **0.605** (121–79) | 0.536–0.670 | 28 / 65 / 7 | 220 vs 159 |

In match 1 the PORYGON2 leaf served 637,248 evaluations and there were 0 errors. The leaf costs about
0.85 ms, so at an equal clock the PORYGON2 arm fills roughly half as many playouts per decision. **No
strength difference is detectable at n = 200.** A +5-point effect would need about 1,500 games.

In match 2 the PORYGON2 arm is ahead of MILTANK's default leaf setup (random depth-2 rollouts, then the
heuristic) and its CI clears 0.5. **Read it with match 1, though.** At depth 0 on both sides the two
leaves tie, so the gain in match 2 is most likely from dropping the two random-policy turns, not from
the net. Heuristic depth 0 against heuristic depth 2 was not run, so this confound is not settled. Match
2 served 703,824 PORYGON2 leaves, with 0 errors and 0 warnings.

## 5. Owed

- A val-selected regularisation pass: more dropout or weight decay, or embedding dropout. Epoch 0 won.
- A cheaper facts path for the leaf, such as caching per-pair hits across the cells of one pass.
- Arena control: heuristic depth 0 against heuristic depth 2. This settles whether match 2 measured the net or the depth.
- V1: MSE against MEDICHAM rollout values.
- V2 as a real SPRT on a frozen release once the gate opens.
- Search-improved targets (v*) at M5, which the learning report expects to lift the label-noise ceiling.
