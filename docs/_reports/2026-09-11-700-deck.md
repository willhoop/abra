## One figure could NOT be bound, and was written as words instead

The two speed-tie corner arms read `960 of 961` and `959 of 961`
(`data/verification/game-differential-top-tie-first.json`,
`…-bottom-tie-first.json`, release `b42b81899631`). `engine/docs_scan.js`'s `CITE_RE` only recognises
`data/<name>.json` — **a path with a directory in it is not a citation it can judge** — so those three
values came back UNBOUND on the first scan of the edited page. They are now stated in words ("a single
game still parts in one setting, and two do in the other") with the exact counts pointed at the
technical documentation, which states them against the release they belong to. Nothing was rounded and
nothing was dropped: the reader is told the shape and sent to the document that carries the digits.

This is a real gap in the binding rule, not a defect in the corner artifacts: **no living document can
bind a figure to anything under `data/verification/`.** Reported, not fixed — `docs_scan.js` is
MEASURE's file.
