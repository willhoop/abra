## 2. THE DAMAGE / KO CLASS — TWO CAUSES, NOT ONE

### 2a. Snow and sand raise a **stat**, and this engine gated them on the move's **category** (rows 11, 16)

**The authority.** `data/mods/champions/conditions.ts` has no `snowscape` and no `sandstorm` key
(grepped), so mainline governs. `data/conditions.ts:707-711` is `onModifyDef` — snow gives an Ice type
×1.5 **Def** — and :663-667 is `onModifySpD` — sand gives a Rock type ×1.5 **SpD**. Which of the two a
hit runs is decided at `sim/battle-actions.ts:1676` and spent at :1709:

```js
const defenseStat = move.overrideDefensiveStat || (isPhysical ? 'def' : 'spd');
defense = this.battle.runEvent('Modify' + statTable[defenseStat], target, source, move, defense);
```

`overrideDefensiveStat` WINS. This format has exactly one such move — **Psyshock**, Special, reading
**Def** (derived: the only `statSwap.attackInto` row in `data/tags.json`; `Dex.forFormat(...)` reads
`category: Special, overrideDefensiveStat: 'def'`).

**The defect.** `engine/medicham2-browser.js` read the move's category:

```js
if(phys && field.weather==='snow' && def.types.includes('Ice')) DCH(1.5);
if(!phys&& field.weather==='sand' && def.types.includes('Rock'))DCH(1.5);
```

so on ONE move it was wrong in **both directions at once**: a Psyshock into an Ice body in snow missed
the ×1.5 (we overkilled), and the same Psyshock into a Rock body in sand took a ×1.5 the authority
never applies (we under-damaged).

**The fix.** Gate on `_dKey`, which is already the authority's `defenseStat` (`statSwap.attackInto ||
(phys?'df':'sd')`) and is already what the stat-multiplier chain, Wonder Room and the boost stages
read. Wonder Room was checked and does not disturb it: `Pokemon#calculateStat` swaps the STORED stat
(`sim/pokemon.ts:568-575`) while `getDamage` still names the unswapped `defenseStat` for the event.

**The cost, measured.** Both rows are a Psyshock into a Froslass standing in its own Snow Warning snow:
row 16 is 76 damage on the authority against our 114 — exactly ×1.5 — and row 11 is the same ratio
turning `6/145` into `0 fnt`. **Row 11 changes who is on the field.**

- Probe: `tests/probe_weather_defence_stat.js`. Knob: `MEDI_WEATHER_DEF_BY_CATEGORY=1`.
- Census row: `move` / `statSwap`, *"Snow and sand raise the DEFENCE the hit reads, not the move's category"*.

**The probe's shape, and why it can fail.** Three PSYCHIC moves, so effectiveness and STAB are identical
across every arm and only the pair (category, stat read) varies — `psychicfangs` P/def, `psychic` S/spd,
`psyshock` S/**DEF**. A category-gated wire and a stat-gated wire agree on the first two and disagree on

### 2b. A redirect ONTO the body already aimed at still fires (row 22)

**The authority.** `followme.condition.onFoeRedirectTarget` (`data/moves.ts:6062-6069`) clears
`move.smartTarget` and returns `this.effectState.target` whenever `validTarget` holds. **Neither half is
conditioned on the aim having moved** — only the ANNOUNCEMENT is, and only on the ability road
(`if (this.effectState.target !== target)`, `data/abilities.ts:2347` lightningrod, :4642 stormdrain).
`Pokemon#getMoveTargets` runs `priorityEvent('RedirectTarget', ...)` BEFORE `getSmartTargets`
(`sim/pokemon.ts:836-840`), so a **no-op** redirect still costs Dragon Darts its second body.

**The defect.** `redirectDrawnTo` answered *"did the aim move"*, returning `null` when the winner WAS the
aim — and `null` is what `_aimRedirected` reads:

```js
if(_drawers.length) return _drawers[0]===aimed ? null : {to:_drawers[0],announce:null};
if(_rods.length)  { if(_rods[0]===aimed) return null; ... }
```

The existing wire for the *aim-moved* case (`MEDI_SMART_TARGET_SURVIVES_REDIRECT`, 2026-09-06) was
correct and simply never reached this road.

**The fix.** Return the winner either way, carrying `aimAlready`. The caller skips the `retargetLastMove`
and the announcement when the aim did not move — which is what the authority does — and still records
that a redirect FIRED. The other two `redirectDrawnTo` call sites already test `_dr.to !== <the aim>`
before acting, so a non-null with an unchanged `to` is a no-op for them.

**The cost, measured.** Row 22, turn 5: a Dragon Darts clicked straight at a Follow Me Clefable. The
authority cleared `smartTarget`, fired the whole move at the Fairy and printed `|-immune|p1a: Clefable`;
this engine kept the split and put **both** darts into the partner Tsareena, 147 → 14.

- Probe: `tests/probe_redirect_onto_the_aim.js`. Knob: `MEDI_REDIRECT_NEEDS_AIM_CHANGE=1`.
- Census row: `move` / `redirects`, *"a redirect ONTO the body already aimed at still turns off the dart split"*.
- The census row's three arms read `[132,66]` (no redirect — the control), `[264,0]` (the drawer IS the
  aim) and `[0,132]` (the drawer is the partner). Before the fix the first two were identical.

The probe derives its own fixture, refuses a partner the move is not neutral into (the real game found
this through a Fairy in front of a Dragon move, and a probe reproducing THAT would read the same whether
or not the split happened), and re-reads the authority's four `smartTarget = false` blocks on every run
— **including that the clear sits ABOVE the `return` with no `!== target` test between them**, which is
the claim this fix rests on.

## 3. THE "SPREAD `-unboost`" CLASS IS NOT A SPREAD MOVE (rows 5, 10)

The instrument's class name is `-unboost: a different body`, which is a statement about the LINE. The
mechanism is a **Magic Bounce that was never re-aimed**.

**The authority.** `magicbounce.onTryHit` reflects with
`this.actions.useMove(newMove, target, {target: source})` — a WHOLE move use, not a re-aim. `useMoveInner`
calls `pokemon.getMoveTargets(move, target)` for it (`sim/battle-actions.ts:467`), whose `default:` case
runs the redirect event before returning anything. The drawer is a **foe of the BOUNCER**, which is the
CLICKER'S OWN PARTNER, because `followme`'s handler is `onFoeRedirectTarget`. The authority then rewrites
the `|move|` line it already printed (`sim/pokemon.ts:845-847`).

**The defect.** `bounceOff` ended `return user;` — the clicker, always.

**The fix.** An optional `ctx = {foes, allies, field}` on `bounceOff`, resolved BEFORE the announcement so
the `|move|` line names the right body. `statusMoveTargets` supplies it (it already holds `it/actA/actB`;
`field` was added as a ninth parameter and passed at all three call sites). A caller that does not pass it
keeps the old destination and is **counted loudly** — `MEDFAILS.bounceRedirectCtxMissing`, non-zero today
on the Yawn / phaze / abilitywrite / healdesc / hazard roads (measured: 1 on a staged Yawn). That is
stated as OWED rather than papered over.

## 4. FICKLE BEAM IS A DIE-ADDRESS DEFECT (rows 28, 33)

**The authority.** `ficklebeam.onBasePower` is four lines and its `randomChance(3, 10)` is raised by
`runEvent('BasePower', ...)` from **inside `BattleActions#getDamage`** (`sim/battle-actions.ts:1649`),
under the authority's own comment `// happens after crit calculation` — the crit is
`randomChance(1, critMult[critRatio])` nine lines above, in the same call.

`engine/game_differential.js` wraps `getDamage` as category `dmg` (`around('getDamage', 'dmg', 0)`) and its
`chance(num, den)` re-labels `dmg` to **`crit`**. So on the authority's side the event addresses as
`crit | turn | ficklebeam | <target slot> | 1` — **the SECOND crit-address draw of that hit.**

**The defect.** `rollConditionalPower(a.move.id, rng)` was handed the battle's GENERIC stream and called at
the TOP of the damage step, above the crit block. Two engines drawing one event from two addresses agree by
luck: at p = 0.3 they match on 0.3² + 0.7² = **58%** of clicks. That is why the roster never saw it — its
dice are a pinned constant, so the address cannot matter — and only whole games did.

**The fix, and why it is two halves.** Switching the stream alone would put this draw at repeat index 0 and
the crit at 1, i.e. the two swapped. So arrival 0's crit die is spent FIRST, through the same `_subAddr` the
crit block uses, and the crit block reuses the value instead of drawing again. It is gated on the move
carrying `conditionalPower{when:'chance'}` — one move in this format — so **no other move's die sequence
moves at all**. `subBlocks` and `critChance` are pure reads and hoisting them consumes nothing.

**The cost, measured.** Rows 28 and 33, both a Hydrapple's Fickle Beam, and row 33 is a KO:

```
row 33  showdown  |-activate|p2b: Hydrapple|move: Fickle Beam   |-damage|p1a: Chandelure|0 fnt
        medicham2                                               |-damage|p1a: Chandelure|38/135
```

- Probe: `tests/probe_fickle_beam_die.js`. Knob: `MEDI_CONDPOWER_OFF_ANY=1`.
- Census row: `move` / `conditionalPower`, *"Fickle Beam's chance is the SECOND crit-address draw of the hit"* —
  and it reads the ADDRESS LIST through `midEventDice` / `midEventLog`, not the outcome, because the outcome
  cannot distinguish the two: control `["…|crit|__fb_nochance|p20|0"]`, test
  `["…|crit|ficklebeam|p20|0","…|crit|ficklebeam|p20|1"]`.
- Counter: `MEDSEEN.condPowerOnCritStream`.

**The differential probe spends the whole PP bar** for the same reason: one click proves nothing when two
independent 30% coins agree 58% of the time. RED before, on the authority's line at turn 3; GREEN after,
with all 5 clicks landing and both engines announcing on turn 3 only; RED again under the knob, which
announces on no turn at all.
