## 2. Back two

**Model.** After the leads, 4 unseen members → 6 pairs. `score(P) = Σ θ[species] + w·φ(P)`, φ from the
store *as it stood strictly before the game* (online): population bring rate, pair co-bring lift, lift
given the observed leads, matchup lift vs the opponent's six, exact-team memory, exact-team+same-leads
memory, and bo3 carry-over (same back pair earlier in the series; overlap with earlier brings split by
won/lost). Update = hard reveal filter. Fitted by the coarsening-at-random likelihood (mass on all pairs
consistent with what was revealed), so short games are not dropped — the full-bring subset over-represents
long games.

## 3. Spreads

**Candidate set.** Every legal SP vector, carried factorised: an alive mask per stat (0..32) plus the
budget (`v + Σ other minima ≤ 66`). Prior = uniform over it (the plan's baseline; no store spreads exist).
`known` pins our own.

**Order.** At each move line the Gen 8+ queue has just been re-sorted with current speeds, so the mover's
(priority, speed) ≥ every still-queued turn-start mover's. Priority comes from the sim's
`getActionSpeed` + `FractionalPriority` (Quick Claw / Quick Draw / Custap taken from the log's proc
line). Equal priority ⇒ `actionSpeed(A) ≥ actionSpeed(B)` (ties allowed; Trick Room via the mod's
negated speed). Skipped: turns with After You / Quash / Round / Pledge, Encore landing mid-turn, called
moves.

**Damage.** First hit of each direct damaging move; `[min,max]` of the 16 rolls from the sim's own
`getDamage` after `ModifyType`/`ModifyMove` (Pixilate, Sheer Force...). Evaluated for every alive
(offence SP × defence SP) by decomposition (one probe per value, `modifyDamage` memoised per base damage),
**verified on every call** against direct `getDamage` cells (0 fallbacks in the final runs) and in
`test-xatu-sd.js` on 450 cells. HP displays inverted to exact-HP bands per HP-SP candidate. Faint / Focus
Sash / Sturdy / Endure are lower bounds only. Anything the snapshot cannot carry is skipped by name
(fixed-damage and history-dependent moves, Substitute, typechange, transform, Wonder Room, stored-stat
swaps, Unseen Fist through Protect...).

## 6. Limits and what is owed

- **Spread narrowing is small** (above). v1 is a sound possibilistic set; the posterior over a stat is
  uniform over its survivors. Next: a likelihood over the 16 rolls instead of the interval, a
  population prior over "archetype" spreads once one exists, and a joint (not per-stat) check.
- **Bring uses reveals only.** No soft in-game evidence (who is kept back when a lead is threatened,
  switch timing). Preview (4-of-6) is not modelled; the belief starts at the leads.
- **Order observations** skip `cant` actors and hidden actions; a mid-turn mega's order among megas is not used.
- **The top calibration bin is over-confident** (0.94 vs 0.89, n = 293).
- **Self-play is RandomPlayerAI** — it exercises mechanics humans rarely produce and under-samples the
  common ones; the human run covers the realistic distribution for consistency but not truth.
- XATU does not touch MEDICHAM; it reads the Showdown checkout directly. When the MEDICHAM `dmgRange`
  API lands, the damage check could switch to it — it must then pass `test-xatu-sd.js`'s cell check.
